Ciko — the shipped default

Ciko is a deadpan house gecko (cicak) in sablon on the pasar palette. Plump bean body, two dot eyes, no mouth, four stubby mitten feet, one accent carrier: the tail tip.

Ciko ships with the skill. Say /lukis install ciko character pack to pin it, or just say "lukis".

Community packs

Community packs live in madearga/lukis-characters — Ciko is the first. Machine-readable list: characters.json.

Build your own

Say /lukis build a character for the interactive builder. A pack is one mascot plus one look — design the locked silhouette, face, limbs, accent carrier, and interaction model, then calibrate it in its look. Publish by PR to the catalog repo once it exists.

Skill repo: madearga/lukis-skill.
