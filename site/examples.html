Style calibrations

Each bundled look is pinned by a calibration render: the line density, negative space, and accent restraint agents should match.

Sablon: flat spot-color inks, one thick even outline, bare-paper seams, accent on the tail tip only.

Riso: halftone grain, ink-layer offset, paper grain under everything.

Woodcut: carved chipped edges, gouge lines, one accent ink off-register.

Chalk: bold dry-edged strokes on deep slate, ghost smudges, no frame.

Enamel: closed metal-bounded cells, one metal color, no pin-back.
